Questions about home-service website design

Can you improve my current contractor website?

Yes. Share its URL and the change you need. A focused improvement starts at $299, with scope and access requirements confirmed before work begins.

Do I need a separate page for every town?

Start with accurate service-area information and useful pages for distinct services. A town page should have a real purpose and specific information, rather than repeating the same copy with a new place name.

Will the website automatically book work?

The starting website includes an inquiry form. Scheduling, payments and AI answering need separate integrations and a confirmed scope; a submitted inquiry does not confirm a booking.
